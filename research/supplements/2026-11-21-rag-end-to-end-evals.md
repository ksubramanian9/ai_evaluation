# Research supplement: rag-end-to-end-evals

As of 2026-11-21. Web access worked (arXiv abstract and HTML full-text pages).

## Claims

- **Yu et al. survey (arXiv 2405.07437).** Separates retrieval targets (relevance: documents vs query; accuracy: ranking relevant over candidates) from generation targets (relevance: response vs query; faithfulness: response vs relevant documents; correctness: response vs sample answer). Auepora = Target, Dataset, Metric. Section 2: "the entire system's performance cannot be fully understood by evaluating each component in isolation". Section 4: with strong LLMs it "becomes challenging to distinguish the impact of retrieval components"; calls for research on "the relationship and analysis between retrieval metrics and final generation outputs". The survey does not give an attribution method. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07437 (read: full HTML via summary).
- **RAGChecker (arXiv 2408.08067).** Claim-level diagnosis with an LLM extractor and entailment checker. Overall precision/recall/F1 vs ground-truth claims. Retriever: claim recall (ground-truth claims entailed by retrieved chunks), context precision. Generator: faithfulness, relevant and irrelevant noise sensitivity, hallucination (incorrect claims not entailed by any chunk), self-knowledge, context utilization. Findings: "As retriever claim recall increases, all generators become more sensitive to such noise"; larger top-k and chunk size raised claim recall and noise sensitivity. Meta-eval on 280 pairwise instances: Pearson 61.93 vs best baseline 48.31 (RAGAS answer similarity), human-human 70.09. https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08067 (read: full HTML via summary, first 100k of 106k chars).
- **RGB (arXiv 2309.01431).** Four testbeds: noise robustness, negative rejection, information integration, counterfactual robustness. Five external documents per question labelled positive or negative; noise ratio 0 to 0.8; negative-rejection testbed contains only negative documents. ChatGPT (2023): accuracy 96.33% to 76.00% (English) as noise rises; rejection 24.67% exact match; counterfactual: accuracy 9 with counterfactual documents vs 89 without. Error types: long-distance information, evidence uncertainty, concept confusion; integration errors: merging, ignoring, misalignment. https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.01431 (read: full HTML via summary).
- **CRAG (arXiv 2406.04744).** Scores perfect 1, acceptable 0.5, missing 0, incorrect -1; truthfulness = average; "prefer missing answers to incorrect ones". Auto-eval hallucination rate is the share of incorrect answers; truthfulness = accuracy minus hallucination. Tasks: 5 pages; 5 pages + mock KG APIs; 50 pages + APIs. GPT-4 Turbo LLM-only 33.5% accuracy; best straightforward RAG 43.6% accuracy (13.4 truthfulness); no straightforward RAG exceeded 20% truthfulness, attributed to retrieval noise inducing hallucinations. Industry systems: hallucination 16-25%, best perfect rate 62.6%. Auto-eval with ChatGPT and Llama 3 70B judges, F1 94.7% and 98.9% vs human labels. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04744 (read: full HTML via summary).
- **RAGAS (arXiv 2309.15217).** "Reference-free evaluation"; dimensions: retrieval's ability "to identify relevant and focused context passages", LLM's ability "to exploit such passages in a faithful way", generation quality. https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15217 (read: abstract only).
- **ARES (arXiv 2311.09476).** Context relevance, answer faithfulness, answer relevance; fine-tunes lightweight LM judges on synthetic data per component; uses a small human-annotated set for prediction-powered inference. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09476 (read: abstract only).
